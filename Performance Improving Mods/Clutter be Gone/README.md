### What The Mod Does
Alternative download: [Gamebanana](https://gamebanana.com/mods/722853)

Removes clutter and improves performance by disabling various particles.

If you're unsure what to download, get this: [all/pak02_dir.vpk](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sqooky/OptimizationLock/refs/heads/main/Various%20Addons%20Relating%20to%20Performance/Clutter%20be%20Gone/all/pak02_dir.vpk)

Explanation of different folders:

| file | contains | description |
| - | - | - |
|`all`| Everything except those marked. | Recommended: With this, you get all useful Clutter Be Gone mods in a single .vpk! |
|`all-no-abilities`| `all` but without `abilities` | For those who want the old Clutter Be Gone, before abilities. |
|`abilities`| `clean_door` | Major cleanup of all heroes abilities effects and some gun and ambient hero effects as well. | 
|`screenspace_city`| `screenspace` and `city_ambient` | A less extreme options, for those who want abilities, movement and NPCs to look the same. |
|`screenspace`| - | Removes screenspace (overlay) effects like low-health, burning, vents smoke overlay. These cost a lot of performance! |
|`city_ambient`| - | Removes ambient effects from the city, such as candle flames, street lamp effects, shop lights, misc bridge buff particles (the bright beam stays), some fan particles, crows, industrial smokestack effects, healing bat dust, etc. | 
|`clean_door`| - | Cleans up The Doormans door. Original: [Clean Doorway](https://gamebanana.com/mods/679758), reimplemented with permission from the author. |
|`walker`| - | Removes lots of particles from the walker, especially the burning fireball effects. Less flashy laser etc. |
|`pickups`| - | Removes the beam of light when you pick up souls/buffs, and removes some particles from breaking containers. |
|`movement`| - | Removes movement dust, the blue air-jump and down-dash rings, walljump and dash-jump particles. |
|`remove_dash_trail`| Not part of `all`! | Removes the blue trail from air-dash/down-dash/dash-jump/air-jump. |
|`unplayable_*`| Not part of `all` | DO NOT USE IN ONLINE MATCHES! Removes ALL particle effects. Invisible bullets, abilities, and more. |

### Installation
Download one or multiple of the zip files and place the vpk files they contain in your addons folder in `Deadlock\game\citadel`, just like you would any other mod.
You will need to rename them to follow the naming scheme of ``pakXX_dir.vpk`` with XX being a number. For example ``pak73_dir.vpk``

Make sure you have this line in your gameinfo.gi file, inside the searchpaths bracket: ``Game    citadel/addons``
